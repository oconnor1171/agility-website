Frequently Asked Questions

How is Agility different from a bookkeeper?

A bookkeeper records what already happened. Agility goes further: we normalize your numbers, benchmark them against the industry average and the best operators in your field, and give you a prioritized plan to improve margin and cash flow. It’s CPA-led diagnostics, not just data entry.

What is operational benchmarking?

Operational benchmarking measures your key financial and operating metrics against both the industry average and best-in-class performers in your specific industry, so you can see exactly where you stand and what to fix first.

What industries do you work with?

We serve Maryland small businesses across many industries, with particular depth in construction and contractors (job costing, subcontractor and margin analysis) and restaurants (prime cost, food and labor benchmarking).

Do you work with businesses outside Baltimore?

Yes. We’re based in Baltimore and work with businesses throughout the state of Maryland.

Is Agility led by a licensed CPA?

Yes. Agility Accountants & Advisors is led by Robert O’Connor, a licensed Certified Public Accountant (CPA).
