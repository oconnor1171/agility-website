Questions owners ask

Do I need QuickBooks or any accounting software?

No. We build your books from your bank statements, card statements, and cash records. If you already use QuickBooks Online, we work from it. You receive finished monthly statements; there is nothing for you to buy or maintain.

I already have a bookkeeper. Can I still get the analysis?

Yes. That is what Insight is for. Your bookkeeper keeps the books, and we deliver the benchmark, ratios, trends and advisory brief from them, monthly or quarterly.

How do you set my price?

By monthly transaction volume and number of accounts. We confirm it from one month of your statements before you commit.

Why does cash matter?

Cash leaves no bank record, so each cash payment needs a receipt or note and counts as a transaction. Businesses that run on cash weekly are priced one level up.

What is the difference between Basic and Advanced?

Basic keeps accurate monthly books and benchmarks your last 12 months once, at onboarding. Advanced repeats the benchmark every month, puts a dollar value on every gap, adds ratios, trends and a monthly advisory brief, and includes a 30-minute quarterly review call.
