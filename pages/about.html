About Us

Our Story

Agility Accountants & Advisors is led by Robert O'Connor, a Maryland CPA with more than 25 years in financial leadership, ERP implementations and business process improvement. Robert built the systems Agility uses to run his own businesses across three industries, so the advice comes from an owner-operator, not only from a textbook. The firm is based in Bel Air, Maryland and works with owner-operated businesses in Maryland, Virginia, Washington, DC and Pennsylvania.

We believe that every business deserves expert guidance from professionals who understand their industry, their challenges, and their goals. Our mission is to help our clients reduce their tax liability, build sustainable businesses, and achieve their financial objectives through strategic planning and expert advice.

Robert works with each client directly, supported by our bookkeeping staff. We take the time to understand each client's unique situation and develop customized solutions that work for them.
