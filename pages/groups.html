Agility Owners Group: now forming

We are starting a small group for owners of construction, restaurant, real estate and service businesses in Maryland, Virginia, Washington, DC and Pennsylvania. The plan: a monthly working session on one financial topic, a place to ask questions between sessions, and plain-language notes you can use the same week.

What founding members get

  • A say in the first topics and the meeting format
  • Monthly sessions on cash flow, job costing, prime cost and tax timing
  • Owners in similar businesses to compare notes with
