Services

Financial analysis and advisory, fractional CFO services, bookkeeping and tax for owner-operated businesses in Maryland, Virginia, Washington, DC and Pennsylvania

Book a Complimentary 30-Minute Call

Financial Analysis and Advisory

We turn your books into comparable operating measures, benchmark them against businesses in your industry, and walk you through what the numbers say and what to look at next. A good first step is the complimentary Benchmark Snapshot.

  • Income statements organized for decisions
  • Industry benchmarking and ratio analysis
  • Regular review of results with the owner

Fractional CFO

Ongoing financial leadership for owners who need a CFO's view of the business but not a full-time CFO. Each month you receive a written Monthly Advisory Brief from a Maryland CPA: a scorecard of where the business stands, what is working, the one issue that matters most right now, and what to focus on over the next 30 days. We then review it with you, so your decisions are made with current numbers.

  • Monthly Advisory Brief, written for the owner rather than for accountants
  • Your results compared with businesses in your industry
  • A regular review of results with you
  • Built on a reliable monthly close; if the books need work first, we start with a cleanup

Scope and fee are set in writing after the complimentary 30-minute call. Book the call

Bookkeeping: cleanup, then a monthly close

Most engagements start with a cleanup that brings your books current and reconciled. From there we move to a monthly close, so you have financial statements you can rely on each month instead of once a year at tax time.

  • Cleanup of past periods and account reconciliations
  • Monthly bookkeeping and close
  • Financial statement preparation
  • Management reporting

Tax Planning

Tax planning for owners and their businesses, done during the year rather than after it ends. We look at how the business is structured, how the owner is paid, and what is coming up, then lay out the choices for you to decide on. Estate and succession planning is handled on the tax side only: we work alongside your estate attorney and financial advisor, and we do not draft legal documents or recommend investments.

  • Year-round tax planning for the owner and the business
  • Owner compensation and entity tax considerations
  • Tax treatment of owner retirement contributions and charitable giving
  • Estate and gift tax planning, coordinated with your attorney
  • Business succession and transfer tax planning

How our tax planning process works

Tax Preparation

Individual and business tax returns prepared by a Maryland CPA. When we also keep your books, the return is built from the same reconciled numbers you run the business on.

  • Individual tax return preparation
  • Business and corporate tax returns
  • Quarterly estimated tax planning

Business Formation and Reorganization: the Tax Side

Choosing or changing an entity has tax consequences that last for years. We help you compare the tax side of the options, including whether an S corporation election fits, and coordinate with your attorney, who handles the legal formation documents.

  • Tax comparison of entity choices
  • S corporation election review
  • Tax implications of restructuring an LLC or corporation
